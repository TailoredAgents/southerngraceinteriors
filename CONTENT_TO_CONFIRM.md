# Content to Confirm Before Public Launch

No unverified founder, location, history, award, review, registration, inventory, or service-area claim is published. Confirm the following with the owner before a public launch.

## Brand and business

- Official business name and registration status
- Preferred wordmark treatment and any existing brand assets
- Founder/owner name, biography, portrait, and personal design perspective
- Business email, phone, studio/showroom details, and service area
- Social accounts and official domain

## Design services

- Final service names, scope, minimum investment, geography, timelines, and inquiry qualification questions
- Real project photography with documented usage rights, project names, and approved descriptions
- Permission for any client quotes, press mentions, credentials, or awards

## Shop

- Real products, vendors, SKUs, photography rights, pricing, variants, inventory, lead times, and fulfillment rules
- Countries/regions served, taxes, freight and white-glove delivery rules
- Final returns, cancellations, damages, warranties, privacy, accessibility, and terms language reviewed by qualified professionals

## Integrations and launch

- Shopify store and Storefront API access
- Sanity project and editorial workflow
- Inquiry delivery destination, consent language, spam controls, and data-retention rules
- Production domain, analytics/consent requirements, SEO indexing approval, verified organization data, and social preview review
